# Module 14: Ethical AI

**Phase 5 -- Specializations**

---

## Description

Build responsible AI systems with fairness, transparency, and compliance. This module covers bias detection and mitigation, fairness metrics, explainability techniques, regulatory frameworks (EU AI Act, NIST), and AI incident response. Ethical considerations are a professional obligation for every AI engineer.

## Learning Objectives

- Detect and mitigate bias in training data, model outputs, and system behavior
- Apply fairness metrics to evaluate and improve AI system equity
- Implement explainability (XAI) techniques to make AI decisions interpretable
- Navigate regulatory frameworks including the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF
- Design AI incident response processes for when systems fail or cause harm

## Prerequisites

- Phase 2 completion ([Module 03](../03-rag/), [Module 04](../04-tool-use-mcp/), [Module 05](../05-model-operations/))
